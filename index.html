Questions

The answers a partner asks for.

Is this legal advice?

No. LabourLedger is a document automation and audit-support tool for qualified professionals. The firm reviews the facts, citations and recommendations before sending anything to a client. The signing professional remains responsible for the final advice.

Does client data leave our browser?

No. Audits and client data are stored in the browser on the firm's device. There is no cloud database, no account and no sync. You can export a JSON backup at any time.

How accurate are the state-specific rules?

The engine covers the central Codes and flags state-specific items such as Professional Tax, Labour Welfare Fund, minimum wages, establishment rules and contract-labour thresholds. State rules change; the report includes a verification checklist and should be reviewed by the firm before issue.

Can we put our own logo and reviewer on the report?

Yes. The firm licence includes a white-label cover, brand colour, reviewer name, designation and membership number. The software brand is not shown on the client-facing report.

What happens when the rules change?

The report cites the Act and the relevant provision. The firm licence includes rule updates and a versioned changelog. If a citation or threshold changes, the firm can regenerate the report with the current version.

Is this a replacement for our labour-law consultant?

No. It replaces the drafting and arithmetic, not the judgement. The report is designed to make the consultant's review faster and the firm's advice more consistent.
