# LabourLedger — launch and sales plan

**Status:** product built and tested; deployment pending the `omkaryele10` Vercel token and a payment link.  
**Wedge:** a white-label Labour Code readiness report for CA/HR/labour-law firms, produced in under 30 minutes.  
**First sale:** ₹999 per report or ₹9,999 for the firm licence.

---

## 1. Who we sell to

**Primary buyer:** partner or senior manager at a 5–50 person CA firm in India with 20–200 SME clients.

**Secondary buyer:** HR consultancy, labour-law boutique, or compliance-as-a-service firm serving SMEs.

**Trigger:** the four Labour Codes are live from 21 November 2025. Clients are asking what changed. The firm needs a repeatable audit product, not another Word template.

**Where they are:**
- LinkedIn: CA firms, labour-law partners, HR consultants in Pune, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Delhi NCR, Ahmedabad, Chennai.
- CA associations, study circles, practice groups.
- HR/ labour-law communities and webinars.
- Existing referral networks of the user.

## 2. The offer

**Pilot:**
- 3 client audits, free.
- One blind audit against the firm’s current junior/Word process.
- Success bar: 90 minutes of partner review by client 3, 45 minutes by client 10.
- If it misses, the firm walks.

**Paid:**
- ₹999 per report.
- ₹9,999/year per firm, unlimited audits, white-label, updates, export.
- Prices exclusive of GST.

**Deliverable:**
- 7-page white-label PDF: cover, executive summary, applicability, 50% wage analysis, 25-point matrix, findings, 30/60/90 plan, calendar, evidence checklist, sources, disclaimer, reviewer sign-off.

## 4. Objection handling

| Objection | Response |
|---|---|
| “My junior already does this.” | “Good. The pilot is blind against your junior’s output. If the draft does not cut your review time, walk. You keep the sample.” |
| “I need to trust the legal content.” | “You review and sign. Every line cites the Act/chapter; the 50% provision is s.2(y). We do not replace your judgement. Have your labour-law consultant review the first three. If the citations do not hold, we stop.” |
| “My clients won’t pay for an audit.” | “Then use it internally to protect the work you already bill. The report is a margin and defensibility tool, not a new product line. The pilot measures review time, not new revenue.” |
| “ChatGPT can do this.” | “For a one-off draft, maybe. In our test, chat runs disagreed by a factor of 12 on gratuity and produced three different citation sets. The app is deterministic, white-label and local. Run the same client through both and compare.” |
| “I don’t want client data in another tool.” | “It stays in your browser. There is no cloud database. Export a JSON backup whenever you want.” |
| “The price is too high.” | “One client audit covers the year. Start at ₹999 per report; move to the firm licence when you have three clients.” |

- Outreach sent: 50.
- Replies: 10%.

- Pilots started: 3.
- Paid reports: 1.
- Annual licences: 1.

## 8. Truths

- A pilot is not a sale.
- The first ₹999 invoice is the only validation that matters.
- Chat is a feature, not the product.
- The app must be sold as a workflow + calculation + white-label system, not “AI writes your audit.”
- Legal content must be reviewed by a qualified professional.
- If the pilot does not beat the firm’s current process, kill it.
