Privacy

Client data stays on your device.

LabourLedger is a local-first web app. There is no account, no cloud database, and no analytics script watching what you type.

What we store

Firm details, reviewer details, client names, wage data, assessment answers, document checklists, notes and licence state are stored in your browser's local storage on the device you use. They are not transmitted to our servers.

What leaves your device

  • PDFs: generated by your browser. If you email or share the PDF, your email provider or messaging app handles delivery.
  • Licence purchases: if you buy a licence, the checkout provider and payment processor handle the transaction under their own privacy policies.
  • Hosting logs: our static host may record standard request logs such as IP address, user agent and page requested. We do not use them to identify you.

Cookies and analytics

LabourLedger does not use advertising or analytics cookies. Browser storage is used only to remember your audits, settings and licence state.

Your client's data

The firm is responsible for obtaining the authority to process client payroll and HR data. LabourLedger is a drafting tool; it does not upload that data to a model or a cloud service. Export backups with care — the JSON file contains client data in readable form.

Deleting your data

Open Settings → Data → Reset all data to delete everything LabourLedger has stored in this browser. You can also clear site data in your browser settings. Export a backup first if you need a copy.

Children

LabourLedger is a professional business tool intended for adults. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
